Individual headword URLs, source snapshot SHA-256 hashes, pronunciation excerpts
and original accent tags are in technology-sources.json. The linked Kaikki pages
link to the corresponding Wiktionary entries and contributor histories.

Modifications by Cloudy's Gloss: select technology-related headwords; retain
broad IPA including stress; omit notes, abbreviated and unsupported regional
transcriptions; deduplicate variants; normalize lookup keys to lowercase; retain
US/UK attribution only where explicit; keep unlabelled pronunciations separate;
only fill gaps in the base dictionaries. Suspect entries are excluded as recorded
in technology-sources.json. No pronunciations were generated by an LLM.

This attribution applies to the supplement data, not to the separately licensed
base dictionaries or application code. No audio recordings are bundled.
